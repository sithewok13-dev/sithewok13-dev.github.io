Sithewok's Game Ports

Unofficial fan ports of classic PC games, with touch controls, for iPhone and iPad.

Made for iPhone and touchscreens first. These ports are mainly for iOS, played with the on-screen touch controls, and that's where they're tested.

Game controllers may work, but controller support hasn't been tested.

Anything else (Android and computer builds) is an experiment: mostly untested, and it may not work.

Bring your own game. No game files are included or shared here: each app needs your own copy of the game, and each guide says how to copy it in.
