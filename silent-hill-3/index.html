Silent Hill 3 for iPhone: build your own

Silent Hill 3 on iPhone, running natively (no emulator), built for free from your own PC copy of the game.

You need your own copy of Silent Hill 3 for PC. The builder contains nothing of the game.

Install

You make the app yourself, for free, from your phone: make your own private copy of the builder, give it a link to your game, and GitHub builds the app for you. The builder's page walks you through it step by step.

Cool features

  • Native, not emulated: the PC game's own code turned into iPhone code, so it runs smoothly.
  • 60 frames a second, held steady with SilentHill3.FusionFix's frame pacing fix. Or 30, for a cooler phone.
  • Full-screen widescreen that fills your phone's screen.
  • Touch controls made for the game: a stick appears under your left thumb (push past its ring to run), drag to look, and big FIRE, AIM and ACTION buttons. A quick tap does ACTION.
  • Modern or classic controls: the stick points where Heather goes, or the original tank controls.
  • Free camera: swap the fixed camera angles for a camera over Heather's shoulder that you turn with your thumb.
  • Photo camera: take photos, bursts and selfies (the game freezes and the camera swings round to face Heather), or freeze a moment and walk the camera round it. Photos go to your Photos app.
  • A cheat: hold the Halo of the Sun button and Heather can't die.
  • Controllers: Xbox, PlayStation and MFi controllers work; the touch buttons hide while one is connected.

This is an unofficial fan project and the iPhone version is very new, so please expect bugs. It's little tested on an iPhone and nobody has played it all the way through yet: it may crash or draw something wrong in places we haven't reached. Things we know about:

There's no multiplayer to worry about (it's a single-player game). Sorry about the rough edges. If something goes wrong, please open an issue and attach sh3.log (and sh3-crash.log, if there is one).

The game's movies (FMVs) are skipped for now.

After the cutscene in the Office Building bathroom the screen can stay black: press ACTION to carry on.

The Konami logos and the credits are stretched on a wide screen.

The photo camera's frozen views can have gaps where the game never drew anything.
