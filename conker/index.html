Conker's Bad Fur Day for iPhone: build your own

Conker's Bad Fur Day on iPhone, recompiled into native iPhone code (not an emulator), built for free from your own N64 ROM.

You need your own US Conker's Bad Fur Day ROM, dumped from your cartridge. The builder contains nothing of the game.

Install

You make the app yourself, for free, from your phone: make your own private copy of the builder, give it a link to your game, and GitHub builds the app for you. The builder's page walks you through it step by step.

Build your own

Cool features

  • Native, not emulated: the N64 game recompiled into iPhone code (CBFD-Recompiled, N64Recomp, RT64 on Metal).
  • Touch controls: a move stick under your left thumb, drag to look, a right stick for the camera, A, B, the C buttons, Z, L and R.
  • Gyro aiming, button opacity and a D-pad, all in START's tray.
  • 60 or 120 frames a second, the in-between frames made smooth.
  • The intro skipped, and a tap skips any cutscene.
  • Controllers (Xbox, PlayStation, MFi) work too.

This is early, so please expect bugs. It has only been lightly tested on an iPhone so far. Sorry for anything that goes wrong; please report it here. There's no multiplayer in this build.
