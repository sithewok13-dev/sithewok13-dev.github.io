Silent Hill 2 for iPhone: build your own

Silent Hill 2 on iPhone, running natively (no emulator), built for free from your own PC copy of the game.

You need your own copy of Silent Hill 2 for PC. The builder contains nothing of the game.

Install

You make the app yourself, for free, from your phone: make your own private copy of the builder, give it a link to your game, and GitHub builds the app for you. The builder's page walks you through it step by step.

Cool features

  • Native, not emulated: the PC game's own code turned into iPhone code, so it runs smoothly.
  • The Enhanced Edition built in: its fixes, HD textures, restored sounds and music, and the HD movies if you want them.
  • 60 frames a second, with the Enhanced Edition's 60 fps fixes. Or 30, for a cooler phone.
  • Full-screen widescreen that fills your phone's screen.
  • Touch controls made for the game: a stick appears under your left thumb (push past its ring to run), drag to look, and big FIRE, AIM and ACTION buttons. A quick tap does ACTION.
  • Modern or classic controls: the stick points where James goes, or the original tank controls.
  • Free camera: swap the fixed camera angles for a camera behind James that you turn with your thumb.
  • Photo camera: take photos, bursts and selfies (the game freezes and the camera swings round to face James), or freeze a moment and walk the camera round it. Photos go to your Photos app.
  • A cheat: hold the Pyramid Head helmet button. James can't die, his guns never run dry, and he gets the weapons, ammo, healing items and flashlight.
  • Controllers: Xbox, PlayStation and MFi controllers work; the touch buttons hide while one is connected.

This is an unofficial fan project and the iPhone version is new, so please expect bugs. It plays on an iPhone, but nobody has played it all the way through yet: it may crash or draw something wrong in places we haven't reached. There's no multiplayer to worry about (it's a single-player game). The free camera can sometimes peek through a wall, and the photo camera's frozen views can have gaps where the game never drew anything. Sorry about that. If something goes wrong, please open an issue and attach sh2.log (and sh2-crash.log, if there is one).
