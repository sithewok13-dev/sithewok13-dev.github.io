Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire for iPhone: build your own

Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire on iPhone, recompiled into native iPhone code (not an emulator), built for free from your own N64 ROM.

You need your own USA v1.2 (Rev 2) Shadows of the Empire ROM, dumped from your cartridge. The builder contains nothing of the game.

Install

You make the app yourself, for free, from your phone: make your own private copy of the builder, give it a link to your game, and GitHub builds the app for you. The builder's page walks you through it step by step.

Cool features

  • Native, not emulated: SotE-Recomp turns the game's own code into code for the phone, and RT64 draws it with Metal.
  • Touch controls like the other ports: a stick appears where your left thumb lands, and you drag anywhere else to look. Modern controls (move and strafe, look separately) by default, or the N64's Classic turn-and-walk.
  • Gyro aiming: off, while touching, or always, with SENS for look speed (1x to 4x).
  • A photo camera: tap for a photo, slide sideways for a burst. Photos go straight to your Photos app.
  • A cheat: infinite health, lives, ammo, missiles and vehicle health, a rescue from bottomless falls (Dash is put back on the last ledge he stood on), and LEVEL SELECT.
  • A start card: CONTINUE goes straight back into the game as your last player.
  • Menus by touch: tap a row to pick it, swipe for the D-pad.
  • Game controllers work too.

This is an unofficial fan project and a beta, so please expect bugs. It plays on an iPhone, but iPads and older iPhones haven't been tried. Holding CAMERA for selfies, Action Freeze (slide down) and the cinematic burst (slide up) are still being worked on: the view can land in the wrong place, show copies of Dash, or come out one flat colour. Now and then the screen can flash one colour for a frame. There's no multiplayer to worry about: it's a single-player game. Sorry about the rough edges. If something goes wrong, please open an issue here, not at SotE-Recomp, and attach sote-log.txt from Files > SotE.
